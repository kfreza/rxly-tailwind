The screens

Four screens. No training day.

Each person in the clinic gets one screen built for their job, and nothing they do not need.

Front desk

The whole day on one board

Sittings, floors and rooms across the top; quick booking on the left; the queue in four lanes on the right. The desk never opens a second screen.

  • Book in ten seconds with a phone number
  • Booked, waiting, with doctor, done
  • Every change reaches the television and the phones at once

Doctor's room

The next patient, one key away

The doctor sees who is waiting, calls the next, and has their earlier visits and reports on the same screen as the pad.

  • Call next, skip, or bring someone back
  • Earlier visits and reports beside the pad
  • Video patients appear in the same list

Prescription pad

Written in seconds, printed on your paper

Complaints, findings, diagnosis, medicines, advice and the follow-up date, each a few keystrokes. The print matches your pad, and the patient gets a link too.

  • Every medicine, with doses and timings
  • Fifteen pad designs, or your own layout
  • Printed, and texted to the patient as a link

Patient's phone

Booking and waiting, from any phone

No app. The booking page works on the cheapest phone in the queue, and the status page updates by itself while they wait.

  • Book with a phone number, confirm by text
  • See the live queue and the expected time
  • Open the prescription later from the same link
