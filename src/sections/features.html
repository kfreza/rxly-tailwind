What it does

Everything a chamber does in a day, in one place

Not a pile of modules. Six screens that pass the patient to each other, from the first text to the printed prescription and the cash drawer.

A queue that runs itself

Book by phone, walk-in or link. Every serial gets a text with its time, the television in the waiting room shows who is next, and "Call next" moves everyone along.

A pad that knows every medicine

Three letters and the brand, strength and form are there. Doses in Bangla or English, your own paper, printed or texted as a link.

Video consults, paid before they start

Tick it on a doctor and online patients can choose a call. They pay first, wait in the same queue, and join from a text.

The patient's own page

One link in the text: their place in the queue, live, and later their prescription. Nobody calls the desk to ask how long.

Cash, expenses and the doctor's share

Every fee lands in the day's ledger. Expenses go in, the doctor's percentage is worked out, and the owner sees the day on their phone.

Your name on the door & on the address bar

Your logo on every page and prescription, a booking page in your colours, and your own web address, with the certificate handled for you.
